How this map was made, and what it leaves out

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and carry their photographers' credits and licenses. Building dates come from the city's building footprint file, which estimates many of them. Spot a wrong or closed site? Every popup links to the site's OpenStreetMap entry, where anyone can fix it; the map picks up changes on the 1st of each month. Methodology.
